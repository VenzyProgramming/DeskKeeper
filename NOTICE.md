# Third-party material

## Tu-160M outline

The top-view outline of the Tu-160M bomber in `src/Art.cs` (coordinates of fuselage, wings and stabilizer) was measured from:

- **"Tupolev Tu-160 3-view graphic.svg"** by **Mike1979 Russia**, Wikimedia Commons
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tupolev_Tu-160_3-view_graphic.svg>
- License: **CC BY-SA 3.0** <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>

The SVG itself is not included. The adapted outline (those coordinates in `src/Art.cs`) is shared under CC BY-SA 3.0. Everything else in this repository is MIT licensed.

## Other aircraft

The top views of the Tu-22M3, Su-34, Ka-52, Tu-95MS and Il-76 in `src/Aircraft.cs` were drawn for DeskKeeper from published dimensions (length, span, wing sweep). They are not traced from third-party drawings and are MIT licensed like the rest.

## Everything else

The explosion frames in `src/Assets` are generated by `src/tools/explosion.py` (procedural noise). Icons in the screenshots and the promo video are drawn by DeskKeeper's demo mode. Interface symbols use the Segoe Fluent Icons / Segoe MDL2 Assets fonts that ship with Windows; they are not redistributed.
